Heatwave Monitoring and Prediction

Heatwave are periods of unusually high temperatures that can affect public health, agriculture, water resources, and daily activities. Climate Intelligence helps users understand changing temperature patterns and identify areas that may experience extreme heat.

The platform supports heatwave monitoring by analyzing temperature trends and presenting important climate information in an accessible format. Heatwave prediction can help citizens and authorities prepare for periods of extreme temperature and take appropriate preventive measures.

Heatwave hotspot detection helps identify regions that are more vulnerable to high temperatures. The system also provides heatwave early warnings so that citizens, hospitals, farmers, and disaster management authorities can respond in a timely manner.

By combining climate intelligence, temperature forecasting, hotspot identification, and early warning information, the platform aims to support better awareness and decision-making during extreme heat events.
