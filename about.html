About Heatwave Intelligence

What is a Heatwave?

A heatwave is a prolonged period of unusually high temperatures that can affect human health, agriculture, water resources, and the environment.

Why AI?

Artificial Intelligence helps analyze massive amounts of weather data, identify temperature trends, forecast heatwaves, locate hotspots, and generate early warning alerts.
